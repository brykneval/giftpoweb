Application Purpose & Overview

What is GiftPo and Why Was It Created?

Every gift carries emotional significance, representing milestones, friendships, holidays, and expressions of love. However, over time, people frequently forget who gave them a specific item, when they received it, or what heartfelt note accompanied it. GiftPo was built to solve this problem by providing a secure, private, and organized digital home for all your gifting memories.

💝

1. Preserve Sentimental Value & Memories

Log each gift with photos, heartfelt messages, giver and recipient names, occasion tags (birthdays, weddings, anniversaries), and personal notes. Never lose track of what a gift meant to you.

🤝

2. Nurture Personal Relationships

View an organized timeline of gifts exchanged with individual friends and family members. Strengthen your bonds by remembering past milestones, expressing heartfelt gratitude, and reciprocating thoughtfully.

📅

3. Track Occasions & Special Milestones

Keep all upcoming celebrations, anniversaries, and holidays organized in one place. Receive timely reminders and keep gift ideas ready so you never miss an opportunity to show care.

✨

4. Seamless Gift Receiving & Wishlist Sharing

Eliminate gifting confusion. Accept incoming gifts seamlessly with a single tap, organize wishlists, share joy with your personal circle, and explore inspiring gift ideas tailored to your community.

Google OAuth & Data Transparency

How GiftPo Uses Google User Data

In strict compliance with Google's API Services User Data Policy, GiftPo is designed with minimal data collection, zero third-party data selling, and maximum user privacy.

🔐

Why GiftPo Requests Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0)

GiftPo integrates Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0) to provide users with a secure, instant, and password-free authentication experience. When you sign in with your Google account, GiftPo requests access strictly to your basic identity details to authenticate you and sync your gift journal across your devices.

Requested Scope / Data Scope Type Exact Purpose & How GiftPo Uses It
OpenID Connect
openid 		Authentication Establishes a verified, tamper-proof session to securely authenticate the user inside the GiftPo app.
User Profile
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile 		Non-Sensitive Retrieves your display name and profile picture to create and personalize your in-app GiftPo user profile.
User Email
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email 		Non-Sensitive Retrieves your verified email address to uniquely identify your account, protect your data, and process account recovery and data deletion requests.

Zero Access to Sensitive Google Data

GiftPo strictly requests only basic login scopes. We never access, read, or request permissions for your Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Photos, or Google Contacts.

Limited Use Policy Compliance

GiftPo's use of information received from Google APIs strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

No Data Selling or Advertising

Your personal profile and gifting data are never sold, rented, or transferred to third-party data brokers, advertising networks, or AI model training platforms.

User Data Control & Instant Deletion

You have full autonomy over your data. You can delete your account and all associated gift data instantly from within the app settings or via our Data Deletion Policy.
