Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 13, 2026

Welcome to GiftPo ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website giftpo.com and use the GiftPo mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways when you use the App:

  • Personal Data: Personally identifiable information, such as your name and email address, that you voluntarily give to us when you register with the App using third-party authentication services like Google Sign-In.
  • Profile Data: Information such as your chosen display name, avatar, and your selected country preference.
  • App Data: Information related to your usage of the App, including gifts you create, track, send, or receive.

2. How We Use Your Information

Having accurate information about you permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. Specifically, we may use information collected about you via the App to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Facilitate account login via third-party services (e.g., Google).
  • Allow you to track, send, and receive gifts with other users.
  • Display public gifts in the Explore tab based on your selected country.
  • Improve the App's performance, features, and overall user experience.

3. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information we have collected about you in certain situations:

  • Other Users: When you interact with other users of the App, they may see your display name, profile photo, and information related to gifts you share with them or make public.
  • Service Providers: We use third-party service providers (such as Google Firebase) to power features like authentication and database hosting. These providers have access to your personal information only to perform these tasks on our behalf.
  • Legal Obligations: If we believe the release of information about you is necessary to respond to legal process or to protect the rights, property, and safety of others.

4. Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and personal data. You can delete your account permanently directly within the App via the Settings menu. Please note that this action is irreversible and all your data, including sent and received gifts, will be permanently removed.

5. Security of Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information you provide to us, please be aware that despite our efforts, no security measures are perfect or impenetrable.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time in order to reflect changes to our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will alert you about any changes by updating the "Effective Date" of this Privacy Policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at notepoapp@gmail.com.
