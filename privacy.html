Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 13, 2026 • App Name: GiftPo

Welcome to GiftPo ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website giftpo.com and use the GiftPo mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways when you use the App:

  • Personal & Authentication Data: Personally identifiable information, such as your name and email address, that you voluntarily provide when registering with the App using third-party authentication services like Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0).
  • Profile Data: Information such as your chosen display name, avatar photo, and your selected country preference.
  • App Data: Information related to your usage of the App, including gifts you create, track, send, or receive, along with optional notes and photos you attach to your gift memories.

2. How We Use Your Information & Google User Data

Having accurate information about you permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. Specifically, we use your information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and securely manage your GiftPo account via Google Sign-In.
  • Synchronize your personal gift log, history, and memories across your devices.
  • Allow you to track, send, and receive gifts with your friends and contacts.
  • Display public gifts in the Explore tab based on your selected country.
  • Improve the App's performance, stability, and overall user experience.

GiftPo does not access any private emails, contacts, or Google Drive files from your Google account. We only request basic profile permissions (name, email address, profile picture) necessary for authentication.

3. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information we have collected about you in the following limited situations:

  • Other Users: When you interact with other users of the App, they may see your display name, profile photo, and information related to gifts you share with them or make public.
  • Service Providers: We use trusted third-party service providers (such as Google Firebase) to power essential features like authentication and secure database hosting. These providers have access to your personal information only to perform these tasks on our behalf in compliance with privacy standards.
  • Legal Obligations: If we believe the release of information about you is necessary to respond to legal process or to protect the rights, property, and safety of others.

4. Data Deletion & User Control

You have full control over your data and have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and personal data at any time. You can delete your account directly within the App via the Settings screen (tap "Delete Account"). For detailed deletion instructions or manual requests, please view our Data Deletion Policy.

5. Security of Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures (including SSL/TLS encryption protocols and secure OAuth 2.0 token management) to help protect your personal information.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time in order to reflect changes to our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will alert you about any changes by updating the "Effective Date" of this Privacy Policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at notepoapp@gmail.com.
