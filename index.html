Google Sign-In & Account Authentication

GiftPo utilizes Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0) to provide users with a secure, fast, and password-free authentication experience. When you choose to sign in with your Google account, GiftPo requests access solely to your basic profile details (your name, email address, and avatar photo).

This information is used strictly to establish your verified GiftPo account, protect your data, and synchronize your gift history securely across your devices.

  • Used solely for identity verification & profile sync
  • No access to your emails, Google Drive, or contacts
  • Your personal data is never sold or used for ads
  • One-tap account and data deletion anytime in Settings
